# Case Study: Google Forms → Slack Lead Alerts

**Client Type:** Small business using Google Forms for lead capture
**Timeline:** 25 minutes
**Tools:** Google Forms, Slack API, Make.com
**Cost to Run:** $0/month

### Problem
A coaching business used a Google Form for discovery calls. Leads submitted, but the founder checked responses only twice daily. By the time they replied, 30% of leads had booked with someone else. Speed to lead was killing conversions.

### Solution
Built instant alert system:
1. Google Forms watches for new responses (trigger)
2. Slack message posts to #leads channel with name, email, message, timestamp
3. Optional: Gmail sends instant "Thanks, we'll reply in 15 min" confirmation to lead

**Flow:** Form Submit → Make.com → Slack Alert (60 sec) → Founder replies in 5 min

### Results
- **Response Time:** 4-6 hours → Under 5 minutes
- **Close Rate:** +35% (faster follow-up = higher close)
- **Time Saved:** 1 hour/day not refreshing forms
- **Missed Leads:** 2/week → 0

### Why Clients Love This
- Uses tools they already have (Forms + Slack — both free)
- No new software to learn
- 25-min build, immediate ROI
- Foundation for bigger system: Forms → Slack → Sheets → AI Scoring → Daily Digest (Project 5)

### What Client Gets
- Working scenario + blueprint
- Loom walkthrough (3 min)
- Documentation: how to add more fields, change Slack channel, add Gmail confirmation
- 7 days support
